6 · That's the whole protocol

  • Pages provide: registerTool({ name, description, inputSchema, execute }).
  • Agents consume: getTools() to discover, then call with schema-valid arguments.
  • State stays on the page: the agent used your session, your code, in front of your eyes — no separate server, no separate login.

Who are the agents in practice? Chrome's built-in agent surface (experimental, behind a flag), browser extensions that bridge tools to Claude/ChatGPT (MCP-B), and automation harnesses like Playwright MCP. No stable browser ships document.modelContext unflagged yet, which is why this page loaded a polyfill if needed — see the badge at the top.
